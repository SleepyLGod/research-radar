# Partitioned Classification Workspace

## Scope

Refine only native App presentation. Preserve the accumulated uncommitted work, saved reports,
snapshots, real configuration and previous Apps. Do not call services, read credential values,
send, commit or push. Real classification quality remains a separate acceptance task.

## Display Contract

- Empty results fill the content area; topic/navigation controls stay at the top. Classification,
  ledger and report-list empty states share a small native message rather than a large empty hero.
  Active filters can be cleared without changing topic, facet or saved data.
- Classification uses unframed sections containing individual paper cards, without permanent
  membership connectors or a second graph engine. Multiple memberships produce views of the same
  versioned paper, not new ledger records. Unclassified papers remain accessible.
- Composite section/paper scroll targets distinguish repeated cards. This is transient App
  presentation state, not a new protocol field, persistent configuration or knowledge relationship.
- Keep saved source evidence, classification reasons, failure notices, stop controls and existing
  report/history navigation. No changes to the research pipeline, prompt, public artifacts or delivery.
- Regular settings collapse provider purpose/fees, Keychain/Codex guidance and notification/schedule
  explanations. Provider status, configuration errors and connection-check cost warnings remain visible.
  First-use provider guidance stays directly visible. Notifications and scheduling remain opt-in.
- Fixture labels are explicitly test material. Do not describe synthetic groups as an accepted
  model classification or fix them by inventing new research conclusions in the UI.

## Evidence And Limits

The regression observed an empty ledger occupying only 239 points in a 600-point workspace before
the fix. Full Swift regression passed 380 tests; final focused checks passed 22 tests after the
onboarding and density adjustments. Python regression passed with two existing opt-in skips.
Native hosted captures were explicitly regenerated for the final layout: English/Simplified Chinese,
light/dark, 640/720-point content widths.

The independent QA candidate uses six synthetic papers, two facets, overlapping groups, an
unclassified paper and long English/Chinese titles. Native button checks observed stable topic
control positions across classification/ledger empty states, filter clearing, repeated-paper evidence
and return, and settings explanations hidden by default. Read-only review found one first-use guidance
regression; it was corrected and re-reviewed.

Separate native launches checked the empty report list with no toolbar drop, English/dark
classification and settings, and first-use service guidance directly present rather than hidden
behind the regular-settings disclosures. All four isolated roots retained empty queues and only
local-read engine audit commands. The QA App and helpers exited. Protected Python/Core sources,
prior App executables and fixture artifacts were unchanged; signature, contained-symlink, Mach-O,
native private-path, Ruff, privacy and diff checks passed. The QA bundle intentionally includes its
isolated root, so it is not a shipping privacy/package acceptance.

Help-arrow automation did not reliably expand the explanation. This remains a manual click/focus
check, not a passed interaction or a proven product failure. Exact long-scroll restoration, keyboard,
VoiceOver and multi-display acceptance remain separate. No live organization quality, new resource
benchmark, shipping bundle/DMG or Release update is claimed.
