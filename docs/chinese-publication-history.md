## What Changed

The previous flow reviewed English and then translated it. Chinese prose could not inherit
the English decision, so readable explanations were removed or reduced to isolated claims.
The new daily and single-paper flow reviews structured facts before preparing Chinese,
then reviews the exact Chinese claims and paragraphs against complete original sources.
The original English analysis remains separate, with decisions linked to the actual
reviewed text. Identity, evidence, quantity and non-promoting status rules remain intact.

For an uncached successful run, English uses one Verifier pass. Chinese with structured
facts uses two passes per run, fact review before writing and final review after localization.
Fact-only Chinese readings use one composition call per paper plus the shared claim/display
chunk; existing bound prose uses body translation instead. Daily figure captions can add
one later localization request when figures are selected. These counts do not include Reader,
search, repair or provider transport retries. Invalid required candidates stop at their own
stage rather than publishing fallbacks. Originals and publication candidates are saved for audit.
